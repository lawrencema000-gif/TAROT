Six of Swords Tarot Card Meaning

Keywords: Transition, Change, Moving on, Passage

Upright Meaning

Transition, moving on, release, and journey toward calmer waters. The Six of Swords represents leaving difficulty behind, necessary changes, and movement from turbulent situations toward peace.

Reversed Meaning

Resisting necessary change, unable to leave the past, or the transition is more difficult than expected. You may be refusing to move on or finding that escape is not as simple as you hoped.

Love & Relationships

Moving on from past relationships, transitioning through relationship difficulties, or the journey from conflict to peace with a partner. This card can indicate physically moving away, emotionally releasing the past, or working through problems toward calmer partnership. The Six of Swords promises that if you make the journey, smoother waters await.

Career & Money

Career transitions, changing jobs, or moving from difficult professional situations toward better opportunities. This card often appears when leaving toxic workplaces, relocating for work, or transitioning between career phases. The journey may not be easy and you may carry some wounds with you, but you are moving toward something better.

Astrological Correspondences

Element: Air

Yes or No

Yes. Six of Swords is moving on to calmer waters — yes, the passage out of difficulty leads somewhere better.

Numerology

Six of the suit is harmonious passage — Air carrying Water, the quiet ferry from troubled banks to a calmer shore.
