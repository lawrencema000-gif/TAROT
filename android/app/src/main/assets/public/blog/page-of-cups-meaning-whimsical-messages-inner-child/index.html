Page of Cups Reversed: Spilled Feelings, Blocked Play Reversed, the Page of Cups shows when the inner child goes quiet or overwhelmed. Signs: moodiness, creative blockage, messages missed, fear of looking “silly,” love offered indirectly and then resented when not recognized. Remedies: reduce the audience. Create for one person—or for your past self. Say what you feel in one simple sentence. Swap grand romance for micro-moments of care. In love, reversed can point to mixed signals or emotional immaturity. The antidote is honest, bite-sized truth. Name your longing without theatrics: “I miss us.” “I want more check-ins.” Small cups, filled often.
