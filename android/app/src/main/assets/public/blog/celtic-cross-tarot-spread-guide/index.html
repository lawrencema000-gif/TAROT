What Is a Celtic Cross Tarot Spread? Complete Guide for Beginners

The Celtic Cross is the most popular tarot spread in the world. Learn what each of the 10 positions means and how to interpret your reading.

The Celtic Cross is a 10-card tarot spread that provides deep insight into any question or situation. It's been used for centuries and remains the most popular spread among tarot readers worldwide. The 10 Positions The Present — Your current situation right now The Challenge — What's crossing you or creating tension The Foundation — The root cause or unconscious influence The Recent Past — What's just behind you The Crown — Your best possible outcome or aspiration The Near Future — What's approaching in the next few weeks Your Attitude — How you see yourself in this situation External Influences — How others see you or affect the situation Hopes and Fears — What you're hoping for or afraid of The Outcome — Where this path leads How to Read It Start by focusing on your question. Shuffle the deck while holding that question in mind. Then lay out the cards in the traditional Celtic Cross pattern. Read the story, not just individual cards. The Celtic Cross tells a narrative — past flowing into present, internal meeting external, hopes confronting reality. Look for patterns: Multiple Major Arcana cards = significant life event Many cards from one suit = that area of life is dominant Reversed cards = blocked energy or internal resistance When to Use the Celtic Cross Big life decisions (career change, relationship, move) When you need comprehensive insight, not just a quick answer Monthly or quarterly check-ins on your life direction When a single card pull feels insufficient The Celtic Cross is available in the Arcana app — try it free at tarotlife.app .
