Leo — Astrology Meaning

confidence · creativity · pride · generosity · leadership · warmth · drama

The performer — radiant, generous, born to be seen.

Leo is fixed Fire ruled by the Sun. While Aries lights the spark and Sagittarius spreads it, Leo is the steady blaze at the center — the sign of self-expression, creative authority, and the courage to take up space. Where Leo sits in your chart is where you must shine to feel alive.

Element: Fire, Fixed, ruled by Sun (July 23 – August 22).

Strengths

warmth that draws others in, creative confidence, loyalty to friends, inspires without trying, lives generously

In Love

Romantic, expressive, loyal, and demanding of admiration. Gives lavishly and expects to be appreciated lavishly back.

In Career

Performing arts, leadership, teaching, anywhere visibility and self-expression are valued. Needs recognition to thrive.

In Spirituality

The path of the heart-king. Self-love as service, not vanity. Leading from love rather than fear.
