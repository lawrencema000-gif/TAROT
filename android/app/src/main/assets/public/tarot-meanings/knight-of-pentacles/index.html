Knight of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Keywords: Hard work, Routine, Dedication, Reliability

Upright Meaning

Hard work, routine, dedication, and methodical progress toward goals. The Knight of Pentacles is reliable, responsible, and committed to seeing things through, even when the work is repetitive or unglamorous.

Reversed Meaning

Workaholism, boredom, stubbornness, or obsession with routine over results.

Love & Relationships

Reliable, steady partnership built on commitment and follow-through. The Knight of Pentacles shows love through consistent actions - being there, providing stability, keeping promises. If this represents you or a partner, expect loyalty, dependability, and practical expressions of care. However, ensure that reliability does not become boring routine. Stability is wonderful but relationships also need spontaneity.

Career & Money

Steady career progress through consistent effort, reliable work ethic, or dedication to routine tasks. The Knight of Pentacles succeeds through showing up daily and doing the work without drama or shortcuts. This card favors careers requiring patience, attention to detail, and long-term commitment. You may not advance quickly but your advancement will be solid and lasting.

Astrological Correspondences

Element: Earth · Zodiac: Virgo

Yes or No

Yes. The Knight of Pentacles is methodical, reliable, and steady. Yes, but slowly and through diligent effort.

Numerology

The Knight of Pentacles embodies the mutable, fixed charge of Virgo-Taurus Earth — craft on the move, the patient plowman-knight.
