Eight of Swords Tarot Card Meaning

Keywords: Restriction, Imprisonment, Helplessness, Self-limiting

Upright Meaning

Self-imposed restrictions, mental prison, limiting beliefs, and feeling trapped by thoughts rather than reality. The Eight of Swords represents the blindfold we wear voluntarily, the prison we build in our minds.

Reversed Meaning

Self-acceptance, removing mental restrictions, gaining new perspective, or breaking free from limiting beliefs. You are ready to remove the blindfold and see that you were never truly trapped.

Love & Relationships

Feeling trapped in relationships, believing you have no options, or mental barriers preventing connection. You may feel you cannot leave an unhappy relationship, cannot express your truth, or cannot find love. The Eight of Swords asks: are these limitations real or self-imposed? What would happen if you removed the blindfold and saw your situation clearly?

Career & Money

Feeling trapped in your job, believing you have no professional options, or limiting beliefs about your capabilities. You may think you cannot change careers, cannot ask for what you deserve, or cannot succeed. This card suggests that you are freer than you believe - the prison is in your mind. What would you do if you knew you were not actually trapped?

Astrological Correspondences

Element: Air

Yes or No

No. Eight of Swords is feeling trapped, though the bonds are partly self-made. The answer is no until you look up.

Numerology

Eight of the suit is mental imprisonment — Air turned cage, the eight blades fenced in by belief rather than rope.
