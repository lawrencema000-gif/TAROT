Three of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Keywords: Teamwork, Learning, Collaboration, Skill-building

Upright Meaning

Teamwork, collaboration, skill-building, and creating something lasting through combined effort. The Three of Pentacles represents the satisfaction of mastery, the power of collaboration, and work done with pride and precision.

Reversed Meaning

Poor collaboration, lack of skill, shoddy work, or working alone when teamwork is needed. The project suffers from disorganization or lack of commitment.

Love & Relationships

Building a relationship through teamwork, collaboration, and mutual effort. The Three of Pentacles suggests that you and your partner are working together to create something lasting - perhaps a home, family, or shared vision. Success requires that both partners contribute their unique strengths. If single, this card may indicate meeting someone through work or collaborative projects.

Career & Money

Collaborative success, skill development, or recognition for quality work. The Three of Pentacles is excellent for teamwork, craftsmanship, and projects requiring multiple people with different expertise. You may be learning from mentors, teaching others, or contributing your skills to a team effort. Quality matters more than speed. Take pride in work done well.

Astrological Correspondences

Element: Earth

Yes or No

Yes. Three of Pentacles is skilled collaboration — yes, when the team works together, the work succeeds.

Numerology

Three of the suit is creative collaboration — Earth shaped by skilled hands working in concert, the cathedral built by three.
