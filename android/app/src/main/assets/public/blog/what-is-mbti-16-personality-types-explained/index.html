MBTI Explained: The Core of the Myers-Briggs Framework MBTI sorts preferences across four dichotomies first articulated by Jung and refined by Katharine Cook Briggs and Isabel Briggs Myers. It does not measure skill; it maps your default modes, the habits you reach for under low friction. Energy: Extraversion (E) vs. Introversion (I) Information: Sensing (S) vs. Intuition (N) Decisions: Thinking (T) vs. Feeling (F) Structure: Judging (J) vs. Perceiving (P) Your result is a four-letter shorthand—like INFJ or ESTP—that reflects these preferences. Treat it as a compass, not a cage. Preferences can flex with context, maturity, and stress. The Four Dichotomies, Decoded Extraversion vs. Introversion Extraversion: refuels through interaction, external processing, breadth of engagement. Introversion: refuels in solitude, internal processing, depth before breadth. Sensing vs. Intuition Sensing: trusts tangible data, specifics, what is observable and proven. Intuition: tracks patterns, implications, and what could be. Thinking vs. Feeling Thinking: weighs logic, consistency, and impersonal criteria. Feeling: weighs values, impact on people, and contextual harmony. Judging vs. Perceiving Judging: prefers plans, closure, and visible order. Perceiving: prefers flexibility, options, and adaptive flow. These are not virtues or vices; they are levers. Notice which side recharges you and which side costs energy to sustain. The 16 Personality Types at a Glance Analysts (NT: Intuitive + Thinking) INTJ – The Strategist: vision-driven, systems-minded, decisive planners who optimize for long-range outcomes. INTP – The Theorist: concept engineers who deconstruct problems, prototype ideas, and chase elegant explanations. ENTJ – The Commander: directive, goal-locked organizers who align teams and resources with crisp priorities. ENTP – The Challenger: inventive debaters who pressure-test assumptions and spin up novel angles at speed. Diplomats (NF: Intuitive + Feeling) INFJ – The Guide: pattern-aware empaths who steward meaning, integrity, and long-arc development. INFP – The Idealist: values-first creators who defend authenticity and imagine better futures. ENFJ – The Mentor: charismatic organizers who cultivate growth, cohesion, and shared purpose. ENFP – The Spark: possibility hunters who energize change and connect disparate people and ideas. Sentinels (SJ: Sensing + Judging) ISTJ – The Archivist: meticulous stewards of duty, process, and proven methods. ISFJ – The Caregiver: attentive protectors who uphold stability and personalized care. ESTJ – The Executor: results-focused coordinators who standardize, measure, and deliver. ESFJ – The Host: community builders who choreograph logistics with human warmth. Explorers (SP: Sensing + Perceiving) ISTP – The Technician: cool-headed troubleshooters who master tools and adapt in real time. ISFP – The Artisan: quietly bold makers who blend aesthetic sensitivity with spontaneous action. ESTP – The Maverick: kinetic tacticians who seize openings, negotiate shrewdly, and thrive under pressure. ESFP – The Performer: experiential optimists who craft moments of joy and read the room instantly. Scan for resonance, not perfection.
