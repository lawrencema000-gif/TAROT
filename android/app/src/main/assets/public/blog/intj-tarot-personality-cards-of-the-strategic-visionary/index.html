The Magician doesn’t wait for perfect clarity; it channels enough clarity into decisive action. For INTJs, this means exiting analysis loops by choosing one lever and pulling it cleanly. On the table: wand, cup, sword, pentacle—tools, not talismans. The infinity above the Magician’s head is sustained focus; the garden is cultivated outcomes.
