Rainbow Moonstone vs. Classic Moonstone Rainbow moonstone (a luminous variety of labradorite in the feldspar family) flashes blue, violet, and multicolor fire. Classic moonstone shows softer white, blue, or peach sheen. Both are lunar allies with subtle differences: Rainbow moonstone: amplifies psychic perception, aura cleansing, and protective light. It’s excellent for empaths and energy workers who need clearer signal amidst noise. Classic moonstone: favors emotional soothing, hormonal balance symbolism, and steady intuition. It’s the everyday anchor for mood and dreamwork. Use rainbow moonstone when you’re reading cards or entering liminal states; choose classic moonstone for daily wear, rest rituals, and cycle care.
