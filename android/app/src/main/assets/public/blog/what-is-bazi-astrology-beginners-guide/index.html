What Is Bazi Astrology? A Beginner’s Guide to the Four Pillars Bazi is Chinese Four Pillars astrology: a precise time-stamped map of your elemental makeup and life cycles. In this guide, you’ll learn the Four Pillars (year, month, day, hour), the Day Master, the Ten Gods, and how Bazi differs from Western astrology. Bazi Four Pillars: Year, Month, Day, Hour Bazi means “Eight Characters.” Each pillar contains two characters—one Heavenly Stem (an element) and one Earthly Branch (an animal sign)—producing four pairs for your birth year, month, day, and hour. Together they show innate tendencies (year), core environment and career climate (month), self and spouse dynamics (day), and behavior/intelligence timing (hour). Year Pillar: social field and ancestral/cultural influences. Month Pillar: the “season” of your chart; strongest impact on resources, career climate, and talent. Day Pillar: the Day Stem is your “Day Master,” your core self; the Branch reflects home life and spouse. Hour Pillar: late-blooming talents, children, sub-conscious drives, and long-term wealth vehicles. Charts are read holistically—no single pillar tells the whole story. The elemental flow across the stems and branches reveals strength, balance, and the life seasons you’ll navigate. Chinese Four Pillars Astrology: Elements, Stems, Branches Bazi works with five elements—Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water—in yin and yang forms. The 10 Heavenly Stems are the pure yin/yang elements; the 12 Earthly Branches are the zodiac animals carrying hidden elements. Their cycle stamps every two hours, day, month, and year, generating your eight characters. Heavenly Stems (10): Jia/Yi (Wood), Bing/Ding (Fire), Wu/Ji (Earth), Geng/Xin (Metal), Ren/Gui (Water). Earthly Branches (12): Rat to Pig, each with “hidden” elements that matter as much as what’s visible. Elemental relationships power the analysis: Production (nurture): Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood. Control (govern): Wood ⟂ Earth, Earth ⟂ Water, Water ⟂ Fire, Fire ⟂ Metal, Metal ⟂ Wood. Reading Bazi is balancing these cycles to identify what supports, drains, or restrains your Day Master. The Bazi Day Master: Your Elemental Core Your Day Master is the Heavenly Stem of your Day Pillar. It is the reference point for your entire chart—how you create, relate, lead, and replenish. Strength isn’t moral judgment; it’s about resource flow. A “strong” Day Master has ample support and roots; a “weak” one needs nourishment and protection. Jia Wood Day Master: upright tree energy—growth, structure, persistence. Ding Fire Day Master: candle/firelight—refinement, precision, influence. Ren Water Day Master: oceanic—mobility, strategy, breadth. The job is not to change your Day Master but to harmonize it. You balance by identifying favorable elements (useful “gods”) and avoiding overexertion of already-dominant forces. The Ten Gods: Roles Your Energy Plays The Ten Gods are not deities but relational roles that elements take in reference to your Day Master. They translate raw elements into life themes: output, wealth, influence, resources, and companions. Each appears in yin/yang forms, giving ten distinct “gods.” Resource (Indirect/Direct): what feeds you—mentors, knowledge, rest. Output (Hurting Officer/Eating God): what you produce—creativity, expression, reputation. Wealth (Indirect/Direct): what you control—money, assets, responsibility. Influence (Seven Killings/Direct Officer): what controls you—rules, pressures, authority that sharpens you. Peer (Rob Wealth/Friend): equals and rivals—support networks and competition. A well-tuned chart lets Resource nourish you, Output showcase you, Influence refine you, Wealth reward you, and Peers support rather than drain.
