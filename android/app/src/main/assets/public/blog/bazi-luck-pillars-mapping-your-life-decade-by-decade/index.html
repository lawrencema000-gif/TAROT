What Are Bazi Luck Pillars (Da Yun)? Luck Pillars, or Da Yun, are 10-year cycles that overlay your natal Bazi, shifting the strength and relationships of the Five Elements over time. Each pillar is a Stem and Branch pair—like a moving weather pattern—amplifying or challenging your Day Master and useful elements. These cycles do not replace your natal chart; they modulate it. A favorable Luck Pillar fortifies supportive elements and opens doors. A harsh pillar stresses weak points, demanding strategy and timing. The 10-Year Luck Pillar: Structure and Meaning Every Da Yun covers a clean decade, but influence ramps up and cools down rather than switching at midnight. The Stem (Heavenly) shows themes, mindset, and external tone; the Branch (Earthly) shows embodied arenas—workplace, family systems, geography, health. Top-down: The Stem “colors the sky”—ideas, policies, mentors, brand, reputation. Ground-up: The Branch “roots in soil”—cash flow, teams, routines, body. Read the pillar against your Day Master. If it strengthens your useful elements (yong shen), expect tailwinds. If it feeds your chart’s over-dominant element, build buffers. Direction answers: do your Luck Pillars flow forward (increasing stems/branches) or backward? It’s set by gender and the Yin/Yang of your birth year’s Heavenly Stem. Yang-year births: males move forward, females move backward. Yin-year births: males move backward, females move forward. If your chart starts from the Month Pillar, you count in that direction to reach your first 10-year pillar. Once direction is set, it never changes. This isn’t superstition—it’s a timing convention aligning your personal polarity with the calendar’s flow. Unsure of your year stem polarity? Generate your full Bazi in Arcana [/more/bazi]; the app flags Yin/Yang and sets direction for you. When Your Luck Pillars Start: The Entry Age Formula You do not begin Da Yun at birth. Your first pillar starts after a number of years determined by the gap between your birth date and the next (or previous) solar node, depending on direction. Count days from birth to the next solar term if your direction is forward; to the previous solar term if backward. Convert days to years (1 year = 1 solar year ≈ 365.2422 days, but in Da Yun counting, 3 days ≈ 1 year is a common traditional rule of thumb; schools vary). The result is your starting age for Pillar 1; each subsequent pillar adds 10 years. Because lineages differ slightly in conversion method, use a consistent system. Entering a New Da Yun: What to Do A new Luck Pillar is a climate change. Don’t panic; adapt with intention. Audit your chart priorities: Re-identify your useful elements and the roles they play—wealth, power, output, resource, companion. Note how the new Stem-Branch modifies each. Phase your goals: Front-load moves that align with the rising element. In a Resource-favored pillar, invest in study, mentors, recovery. In Output pillars, ship work and build audience.
