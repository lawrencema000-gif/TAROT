Full Moon Release Tarot Spread

Positions

  1. 1. What has bloomed this cycle — The growth, gain, or completion worth naming and celebrating before anything else. Skipping this card cheapens the rest of the spread.
  2. 2. What is asking to be released — The pattern, belief, relationship, or commitment that has reached the end of its usefulness. Often something you have been postponing letting go of.
  3. 3. What you are afraid to release — The reason the release has not happened yet — the fear, story, or imagined cost holding it in place. Naming it loosens its grip.
  4. 4. The gift waiting on the other side — The space, energy, or opening that becomes available once the release happens. Often the very thing you have been wishing for.
  5. 5. The next gentle step — A small, doable action toward the release — usually emotional rather than logistical, somatic rather than analytical.
  6. 6. What to carry forward — The lesson, gift, or quiet wisdom from this cycle that travels with you into the next. The opposite of release — the keeping.
