Knight of Swords Tarot Card Meaning

Keywords: Action, Ambition, Speed, Determination

Upright Meaning

Ambitious action, mental determination, rushing toward goals, and cutting through obstacles with focused intent. The Knight of Swords is direct, fast, and sometimes reckless in pursuit of truth or goals.

Reversed Meaning

Impulsiveness, aggression, reckless communication, or rushing in without thinking. Speed without wisdom leads to crashes.

Love & Relationships

Intense, fast-moving romantic pursuit, or relationships that develop quickly and burn hot. This card can indicate someone charging into your life with declarations and intensity, or your own tendency to rush relationships. While exciting, the Knight of Swords warns against moving too fast, saying too much, or letting intensity replace depth. Slow down enough to build something lasting.

Career & Money

Ambitious career moves, quick decisions, or charging toward professional goals with determination. This card favors bold action, quick thinking, and cutting through bureaucratic nonsense. However, ensure your ambition does not become aggression, your speed does not become recklessness. Think before you speak, plan before you act.

Astrological Correspondences

Element: Air · Zodiac: Gemini

Yes or No

Yes. The Knight of Swords charges forward at speed — yes, but recklessness may blunt the edge of victory.

Numerology

The Knight of Swords embodies the mutable, fixed charge of Gemini-Aquarius Air — thought on the move, the headlong intellect.
