Uranus — Astrology Meaning

revolution · awakening · innovation · rebellion · electricity · breakthrough · shock

Where the unexpected breaks through.

Uranus rules sudden change, awakening, and the rebellious genius. Its sign is generational; its house shows where your life will see surprises and where you must break with convention to grow.

Strengths

original thinking, liberation from outdated patterns, genius for breakthroughs

In Love

Uranus contacts in synastry = unconventional connections, sometimes love-at-first-sight, often unstable.

In Career

Tech, science, activism, anywhere innovation is the work itself.

In Spirituality

The path of awakening. Sudden satori. The old self, gone.
