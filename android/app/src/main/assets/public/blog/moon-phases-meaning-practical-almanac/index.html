Moon Phases Explained The lunar cycle moves through eight distinct phases in roughly 29.5 days. Think of it as a monthly arc: seed, sprout, build, bloom, harvest, release, and rest. Aligning your goals and check-ins with this cadence brings steadiness to growth and clarity to change. For daily guidance, pair the lunar rhythm with your personal transits via Arcana’s horoscope tools and quick spreads in tarot readings .
