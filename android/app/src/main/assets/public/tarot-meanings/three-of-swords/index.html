Three of Swords Tarot Card Meaning

Keywords: Heartbreak, Sorrow, Grief, Separation

Upright Meaning

Heartbreak, emotional pain, sorrow, grief, and the wounds that words or betrayal can inflict. The Three of Swords represents the moment when truth hurts, when you must feel pain in order to heal, and when the heart breaks open.

Reversed Meaning

Recovery from heartbreak, forgiveness, releasing pain, or avoiding necessary grief. The swords are being removed and healing can begin, or you are refusing to feel the pain that must be felt.

Love & Relationships

Heartbreak, betrayal, painful truth in relationships, or necessary endings. This is the card of breakups, discovering infidelity, or realizing that love alone is not enough. The Three of Swords validates your pain while reminding you that this wound, though deep, will heal. Sometimes relationships must end for growth to occur. Sometimes truth must hurt before it can heal. Your heart is strong enough to survive this.

Career & Money

Professional disappointment, conflicts with colleagues, or harsh feedback that wounds your pride. You may be facing criticism, rejection, or the painful realization that your work is not valued as you hoped. While this hurts, the Three of Swords often brings clarity - you now see situations truthfully even if that truth is painful. Use this clarity to make necessary changes.

Astrological Correspondences

Element: Air

Yes or No

No. Three of Swords is heartbreak and painful truth. The answer is no — and grieve before you continue.

Numerology

Three of the suit is heartbreak — Air pierced through Water, the three blades that name the cost of truth seen too late.
