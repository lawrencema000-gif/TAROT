How to Calculate Life Path from Your Birth Date The Life Path Number comes from your full birth date reduced to a single digit, unless it totals a master number (11, 22, or 33). Use digit-by-digit reduction so every piece of your date contributes. Step 1: Write your birth date numerically (MM/DD/YYYY). Step 2: Sum the digits of the month to a single digit. If 10, 11, or 12, reduce: e.g., 12 → 1+2=3. Step 3: Sum the digits of the day to a single digit (keep 11, 22 as master days only for day calculation context—your Life Path still comes from the full-date total). Step 4: Sum the digits of the year to a single digit. Step 5: Add the three results, then reduce to one digit. If the final total is 11, 22, or 33, keep it as a Master Life Path. Example: 12/25/1990 Month: 1+2=3 Day: 2+5=7 Year: 1+9+9+0=19 → 1+9=10 → 1+0=1 Total: 3+7+1=11 → Master Life Path 11 Tip: Different schools either reduce each segment first (as above) or sum all digits at once. Both usually land the same Life Path. If they differ, favor the segmented approach for consistency.
