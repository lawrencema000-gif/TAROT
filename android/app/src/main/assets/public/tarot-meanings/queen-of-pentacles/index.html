Queen of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Keywords: Nurturing, Practical, Security, Abundance

Upright Meaning

Nurturing abundance, practical care, down-to-earth support, and creating security for self and others. The Queen of Pentacles masters the art of making everything comfortable, beautiful, and prosperous.

Reversed Meaning

Neglecting self-care, smothering care for others, or losing connection to the physical world through overwork.

Love & Relationships

Nurturing partnership, creating comfortable home together, or showing love through practical care. If this represents you, you express love by creating security, cooking meals, making spaces beautiful, and providing for those you love. If this represents a partner, expect someone warm, reliable, and generous with resources. The Queen of Pentacles creates relationships where everyone feels cared for and secure.

Career & Money

Successful business ownership, careers in hospitality, real estate, or any field requiring both financial acumen and nurturing care. The Queen of Pentacles excels at making money while taking care of people. She manages resources wisely, creates comfortable environments, and builds prosperity that benefits everyone. Your practical wisdom and generous nature make you an exceptional leader.

Astrological Correspondences

Element: Earth · Zodiac: Capricorn

Yes or No

Yes. The Queen of Pentacles nurtures abundance and grounded prosperity. Yes — practical and warm.

Numerology

The Queen of Pentacles embodies the cardinal, deep core of Capricorn-Virgo Earth — Earth as the nurturing mother of the hearth and harvest.
