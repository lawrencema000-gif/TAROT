How a Human Design Chart Is Computed A Human Design chart is calculated using astronomical positions at two moments: your birth and approximately 88 solar degrees (about 88 days) before birth. These two timestamps create your conscious (personality) and unconscious (design) imprints. Conscious imprint (black): positions at your birth—traits you recognize. Unconscious imprint (red): positions ~88 days pre-birth—body-level patterns you feel more than see. The system uses a real ephemeris to place the Sun, Earth, Moon, and planets into 64 I Ching hexagrams (gates). Definitions across the nine centers show which energies are consistent (defined) and which are open (receptive).
