Knight of Cups Tarot Card Meaning

Keywords: Romance, Charm, Following heart, Beauty

Upright Meaning

Romance, following your heart, artistic sensitivity, idealism, and emotional quests. The Knight of Cups is the romantic dreamer, the poet and artist who leads with feeling and seeks beauty in all things.

Reversed Meaning

Moodiness, disappointment, unrealistic expectations, or being overly romantic without substance. All feeling without action, or projecting fantasy onto reality.

Love & Relationships

Romantic gestures, declarations of love, or someone pursuing you with artistic sensitivity and emotional openness. The Knight of Cups represents classic romance - flowers, love letters, deep gazes, and heartfelt expressions of devotion. If this is you, you are ready to pursue love with an open heart. If it represents another person, expect someone charming, romantic, and emotionally expressive. However, ensure there is substance behind the romance and the gestures lead to real commitment.

Career & Money

Following your passion in work, careers in the arts or helping professions, or projects that require emotional depth and creative vision. This card favors writers, artists, therapists, and anyone whose work involves beauty, feeling, and human connection. Your idealism and emotional intelligence are professional assets now. However, balance vision with practical execution.

Astrological Correspondences

Element: Water · Zodiac: Pisces
