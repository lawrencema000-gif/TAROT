Career Decision Tarot Spread for Clear Crossroads Choices When your work life stands at a fork, clarity beats bravado. This 6-card career decision tarot spread maps your present, your pull toward change, and the road one year ahead. You’ll learn precise positions, how to read them, and how to turn insight into an action plan. The 6-Card Career Decision Tarot Spread Lay six cards left to right in a gentle arc. Each position pinpoints a decision pressure point—no filler, just signal. Card 1: Current Role — Where you truly stand. Card 2: The Call — What’s beckoning you forward. Card 3: Fears — The saboteurs and real risks. Card 4: Support — Allies, skills, and timing on your side. Card 5: The Leap — The move to make now. Card 6: Year-1 Outcome — Likely arc over the next 12 months. Use your regular deck, cut once with a clear question: “What choice most sustains my growth and livelihood over the next year?” For a fast read, try a quick AI reading to pre-clarify the question, then pull your cards. How to Read Each Position with Precision Card 1: Current Role. Read the honest baseline: workload, learning curve, recognition, and runway. Pentacles show material stability or stagnation; Wands show momentum or burnout; reversed Majors flag a deeper misalignment. Card 2: The Call. This is the magnetic direction—not always a job title, sometimes a value (autonomy, impact, craft). Cups reveal heart-led purpose, Swords indicate a clearer strategy or credential path, the Sun or Star signal green lights. Card 3: Fears. Separate phantom anxieties from practical risks. Nine of Swords is rumination; Five of Pentacles warns of budget gaps; the Tower upright can mean short-term upheaval that clears space. Card 4: Support. Name resources you can actually deploy. Court cards can be mentors; Three of Pentacles is team synergy; Temperance reminds you to pace cash flow and skill-building. Card 5: The Leap. Translate insight into one decisive action: apply, pitch, renegotiate, build a portfolio piece, or set a 90-day exit plan. Ace cards point to starting energy; the Chariot says drive now with focus. Card 6: Year-1 Outcome. Not fate, but a likely trajectory if you act on Card 5. Ten of Pentacles suggests compounding stability; Six of Wands shows public wins; Hanged Man indicates a reflective year where patience pays. Interpreting Common Cards in a Job Decision Tarot Aces (any suit): Greenlight beginnings; pair with a concrete first step. The Devil: Name the golden handcuffs—money, status, comfort—and set release conditions. Justice: Contracts, negotiations, fair pay; get everything in writing. Eight of Cups: A clean, values-led departure; plan logistics before the walk. Two of Pentacles: You can balance two tracks short-term; schedule prevents chaos. Death: Close one chapter fully; don’t half-exit. New growth needs cleared ground. Use elemental patterns for nuance: Many Wands: Momentum matters more than perfect certainty. Many Pentacles: Optimize compensation, skills, and stability. Many Swords: Strategy, messaging, and boundary-setting are the leverage. Many Cups: Culture, meaning, and wellbeing drive the choice. A Sample 6-Card Career Spread Walkthrough Imagine this pull: Current Role — Four of Pentacles: Secure but tight; growth capped. The Call — The Star: A clear, energizing direction. Fears — Five of Pentacles: Income dip worries. Support — Three of Pentacles: Strong collaborators and portfolio proof. The Leap — Knight of Swords: Send bold applications and sharpen your story. Year-1 Outcome — Six of Wands: Public recognition after focused push. Action plan: keep a three-month cash cushion, ship two case studies, line up references, and apply weekly to aligned roles.
