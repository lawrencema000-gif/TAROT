King of Cups Tarot Card Meaning

Keywords: Emotional balance, Wisdom, Diplomacy, Generosity

Upright Meaning

Emotional balance, wisdom, diplomacy, generosity, and mastery of the emotional realm. The King of Cups has deep feelings but controls them with maturity, offering calm guidance and compassionate leadership.

Reversed Meaning

Emotional manipulation, suppressed feelings, coldness disguised as control, or moodiness. The emotional king may become tyrannical or emotionally unavailable.

Love & Relationships

Emotionally mature partnership, wise and compassionate love, or a partner who offers both passion and stability. The King of Cups represents someone who has done their emotional work, who can be vulnerable without being weak, and who leads with both heart and head. If this is you, you offer your partner emotional security, wisdom, and deep compassion. Expect mature communication, balanced emotions, and relationship decisions made from both love and wisdom.

Career & Money

Leadership that balances logic and emotion, careers in diplomacy, counseling, or mediation. The King of Cups excels in positions requiring emotional intelligence combined with authority - therapy, social services leadership, diplomatic roles, or any position where you guide others through emotional complexity. Your ability to remain calm in emotional storms while still caring deeply makes you an exceptional leader.
