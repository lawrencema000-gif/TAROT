How to Calculate Life Path Number The simplest method: reduce your full birth date (MM/DD/YYYY) to a single digit by adding each component, keeping master numbers 11, 22, and 33 intact. Step 1: Reduce the month to a single digit (except 11). Example: December = 12 → 1 + 2 = 3. Step 2: Reduce the day to a single digit (except 11 or 22). Example: 14 → 1 + 4 = 5. Step 3: Reduce the year to a single digit. Example: 1990 → 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1. Step 4: Add the reduced parts and reduce again unless the result is 11, 22, or 33. Worked example: Birth date 12/14/1990 Month: 12 → 1 + 2 = 3 Day: 14 → 1 + 4 = 5 Year: 1990 → 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1 Total: 3 + 5 + 1 = 9 → Life Path 9 Tip: If your sum is 11, 22, or 33, stop reducing.
