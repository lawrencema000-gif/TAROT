Strength Tarot Card Meaning

Keywords: Courage, Patience, Control, Compassion

Upright Meaning

Courage, compassion, inner strength, and gentle persuasion. Strength represents the triumph of the gentle spirit over raw force, showing that true power lies not in domination but in patient understanding and loving courage. This card speaks to mastering your instincts and passions through compassion rather than suppression, befriending your inner beast rather than caging it. Like the figure who calmly places their hands upon the lion's jaws, you possess the courage to face what frightens you and the wisdom to know that force creates resistance while gentleness creates transformation. This is the strength of the quiet voice that speaks truth to power, the parent who guides with patience rather than punishment, the leader who inspires rather than intimidates. Strength asks you to approach challenges with both courage and compassion - for yourself and others. It reminds you that you are stronger than you know, not because you can overpower opposition, but because you can transmute it through understanding and love.

Reversed Meaning

Self-doubt, weakness, insecurity, or abuse of power. You may be lacking confidence in your abilities or conversely, using force where gentleness would serve better. This reversal can indicate that fear has gained the upper hand, that you're avoiding confrontation when courage is needed, or that you're suppressing rather than integrating difficult emotions. It may suggest raw emotion overwhelming reason, or letting your "inner beast" run wild without the tempering influence of wisdom. Alternatively, this card reversed can point to being too passive, allowing others to walk over you, or losing touch with your personal power. You might be doubting your capacity to handle challenging situations or people. Sometimes this reversal indicates misusing strength - being controlling, aggressive, or domineering rather than persuasive and patient. Reconnect with your inner courage, practice self-compassion, and remember that true strength is gentle but unwavering.

Love & Relationships

Patient understanding and gentle strength in relationships. If single, approach new connections with confidence tempered by compassion - be authentic but kind. If partnered, this card suggests working through difficulties with patience rather than force, addressing conflicts with courage but never cruelty. Strength reminds you that love requires the courage to be vulnerable, the patience to allow growth, and the wisdom to accept what cannot be changed. This is an excellent time to have difficult conversations approached with compassion, to forgive past hurts, or to show gentle strength in setting boundaries. The strongest relationships are built not on power dynamics but on mutual respect and tender courage. If there are "wild" aspects of yourself or your partner that cause friction, approach them with understanding rather than judgment.

Career & Money

Leading through inspiration and quiet confidence rather than force or intimidation. This card favors careers in counseling, healing, animal care, conflict resolution, or any field requiring patience and compassion combined with courage. Your ability to remain calm under pressure and to persuade through understanding rather than aggression will bring professional success. This is an excellent time to address workplace conflicts diplomatically, to mentor others with patience, or to advocate for yourself or others with confident grace. Strength suggests that persistence and gentle determination will overcome obstacles that brute force cannot budge. If you face difficult colleagues or challenging situations, respond with composure and compassion - you will be surprised by your power to transform resistance into cooperation.
