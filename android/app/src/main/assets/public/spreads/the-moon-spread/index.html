The Moon Spread Tarot Spread

Positions

  1. 1. What is unclear — The specific area of fog. Not the whole situation — the precise piece that cannot yet be seen. Naming it tightens the focus.
  2. 2. Your fear — The fear coloring your perception right now. Read this card honestly; the Moon will not let you separate signal from noise until you have named the noise.
  3. 3. What is illusion — The specific story or image distorting the situation. Sometimes from outside, sometimes from inside, sometimes both. The fog itself, named.
  4. 4. What is real — The actual fact under the fog. Often smaller and more ordinary than feared. The ground, named.
  5. 5. Your dream guidance — The intuitive or unconscious wisdom available to you. The dog and wolf howling — what your deep self has been signaling through dreams, body sensations, recurring imagery, or quiet inner voice.
  6. 6. The path through fog — The actual movement forward. Not a leap — the next step that can be taken even with limited visibility. The Moon does not promise full clarity, only enough light for the next stride.
