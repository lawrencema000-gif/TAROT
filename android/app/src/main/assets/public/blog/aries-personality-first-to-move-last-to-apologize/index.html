Aries Anger: Flash Fire, Fast Clear Aries anger is weather: sudden, intense, brief. It spikes when they feel blocked, disrespected, or trapped in circular talk. The outburst is heat release, not a grudge engine. Give them honest feedback after the storm, not during it. What works: direct language, short sentences, clear choices. What fails: guilt trips, ambiguity, slow-rolling decisions. If you need time, say so. If you need change, name it. Aries will meet you in resolve, not rumination. Course-correct early, when they’re energized and open to iteration. Match pace, not volume. You don’t have to shout—just be quick and clear. Turn friction into fuel. Convert conflicts into challenges with stakes and timelines. Celebrate the start and the ship. Aries thrives on visible wins—kickoff recognition and launch applause both matter.
