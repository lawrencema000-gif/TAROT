Astrology shows themes, timing, and temperament through planets, signs, and houses. Human Design shows how your energy operates—your reliable strengths (defined centers), consistent traits (channels), and the practical way to move (Strategy and Authority). Inputs: Astrology: birth date, time, place → natal chart of planets by sign/house/aspect. Human Design: the same birth data plus a second timestamp 88 solar degrees earlier (about 88 days pre-birth) → BodyGraph derived from the I Ching, Kabbalah, chakra centers, and neutrino-informed planetary positions. Outputs: Astrology: meaning through archetypes and transits. Human Design: a binary map of definition/open-ness with operational rules—wait to respond, wait for invitation, inform, etc.—plus an inner Authority (sacral, emotional, splenic) to make decisions. If astrology is the weather and terrain, Human Design is the vehicle manual. Use astrology to understand the landscape; use Human Design to operate your engine.
