Citrine Properties Natural citrine is a yellow to smoky-gold variety of quartz colored by iron and heat within the Earth. Its energy profile mirrors its clarity: bright, direct, and energizing without jitter. Chakra affinity: solar plexus (Manipura) and, for paler stones, a bridge to the sacral for creative flow. Elemental feel: Fire with Air—warm initiative plus mental clarity. Practical effects: supports decision-making, sales conversations, public speaking, and resilient optimism after setbacks. Quality indicators: Color: pale champagne to honey-gold for natural; even saturation, not patchy. Transparency: from translucent to transparent; inclusions are fine if color and feel remain warm and consistent. Luster: vitreous, with a clean sparkle that stays bright in different lights.
